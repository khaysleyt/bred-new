About BRED

Building bridgesthrough education.

BRED brings together education, inclusion, European cooperation and innovation to support young people and communities along their journeys.

Our mission

Making education a starting point.

BRED uses education as a driver of development, participation and cooperation.

Its work connects educational guidance, equal opportunities, European openness, and discovery in science and digital technology.

  3. 03

  4. 04

    STEM

    Science, technology, innovation and digital literacy.
